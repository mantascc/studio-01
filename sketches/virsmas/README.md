# VIRSMAS — a field in becoming

[Open the studies](index.html) · [Sketch gallery](../index.html)

A generative sketch about reality in constant becoming. One living field in each of two studies: flow, temporary form and environmental feedback operate simultaneously.

**Woven currents:** movement reinforces paths; slower accumulated fatigue makes those paths less attractive. **Restless territories:** two populations reinforce their own traces and avoid each other’s; shared fatigue destabilizes boundaries. Lines and squares distinguish the populations in black and white.

Drag or touch the field to disturb it. The system continues unattended. Keyboard: focus the field and press Enter to disturb its center, or Space to pause. Pause is also available above the field. Reduced-motion preference starts paused. Hidden tabs suspend without catch-up. Resize preserves history; changing studies begins a new field. Additional reset/seed controls are under About this field.

The model uses 1,900 moving elements, diffusing traces and slower fatigue on a periodic 128×128 field. All elements sense the same prior field before depositing new traces. Seeded beginnings are reproducible; there are no prescribed destination shapes or timed morphs. Feedback changes subsequent trajectories, but this is not a claim of biological autopoiesis. Broad recurring calm/turbulent cycles and physical-phone acceptance remain open observations.

*Virsmas nėra tai, kas nutinka daiktams. Virsmas yra tai, kaip daiktai egzistuoja.*
