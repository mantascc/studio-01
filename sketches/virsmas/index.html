About this field

Flow, temporary form and self-creation operate together. Motion leaves a memory; that memory changes the next motion. No shape is waiting at the end.

Touch or drag to disturb. Keyboard: Enter on the field disturbs its center. Space pauses when the field is focused. The edges connect. Changing studies begins a new field.
