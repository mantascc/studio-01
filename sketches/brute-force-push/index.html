About the sketch

Each collision wears down a square. After pressure subsides, it regrows from a small seed into a solid block. Continued contact delays recovery; openings attract movement and can then close behind it. The wall is never permanently defeated. Turn recovery down to compare one-way erosion against ongoing regeneration.

Space to pause. Escape to release focus. Reduced-motion preferences begin paused. This is a stylized computational study, not a physical materials simulation.
