Pick a rule. Then give it a personality.

Each family contains two kawaii, two playful, two functional and two Bauhaus studies. The eight specimens change topology or construction, not just color. Tap ☆ to save candidates; Compare keeps their idle and working poses side by side.

A common comparison

The four ratings are design judgments, not user research: identity (a distinctive structural signature), expression (clear state poses), 64 px (small-scale legibility) and editing (meaningful room to customize). Five bars is strongest. Read each family’s tradeoff before choosing.

Use the 64 px switch for the actual interface footprint. The laboratory shows the same identity on both grounds and adds internal structure at communication scale. Color stays constant across themes and states.

One file, your decisions

All drawing, controls and motion work offline. Edits and saved studies stay in this browser where storage is available. Export a recipe to keep an identity independently of the browser; import it here to reproduce the result.

Every pose works without animation. Playback is opt-in and stops when reduced motion is requested. State shapes are hypotheses to review, not a validated universal vocabulary.

Construction notes & reference lineage

Computational minimalism supplies the method: a small vocabulary, deterministic rules, meaningful negative space. Clear Channel informs the grid, comparison rhythm and visible mechanisms. Day 89 contributes four readable state poses; Day 30 contributes time as a parameter. Their monochrome and hover-led conventions are deliberately adapted for identity color and touch.

SVG is the common substrate here: even collective and matrix studies remain small vector scenes. Raster sprites, canvas fields and 3D are deferred until a chosen direction needs them. No stock images, external fonts, libraries or network assets are used.
