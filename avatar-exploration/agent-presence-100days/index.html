first read

Provisional design judgments from building these, not user-tested. Scores are 1–4 on identity at rest, state expression, 64 px legibility and meaningful customisation.

strongest

trace · automaton · sprite

Each produces a distinct identity from a seed or a short recipe, and each survives 64 px. Trace and automaton are generative signatures; sprite is the most immediately likeable.

worth a second pass

lattice · orbital · ascii

Lattice and orbital express state best of all. Both need heavier strokes at small sizes. ASCII is the cheapest system but depends on the font.

open questions

is color enough?

In a population of fifty agents, six hues repeat quickly. Shape has to carry most of the identity; color becomes a second key. The matrices page tests exactly that.
